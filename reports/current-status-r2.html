Union Alpha current-status, repeatability, and capacity delta

Run run:manual:8245aa52a11393edf3b6e8fbfc3ccabd · cutoff 2026-09-18T08:26:20Z–08:35:00Z UTC · narrow route/status recheck. This publication summarizes the accepted report; it preserves R1 separately.

Decision brief
OpenRouter’s free stealth preview ended. Its historical page identifies Union Alpha as Pareto by Unbiased and points to paid unbiased/pareto; the public catalog has no old-ID match. OpenCode Go still documents union-alpha, but its subscription/key-gated access was not tested. R2 sent zero inference requests.

Attribution and benchmark boundary

OpenRouter’s reveal and Unbiased’s own attribution resolve the former owner question. They do not reveal Pareto’s component membership or make prompt-fingerprint theories proof. The provider model card claims Pareto 26.9 scores of 74 DeepSWE and 51 Terminal-Bench 4.0; the public harness documents an intended method. The inspected materials did not expose raw runs linking those figures to the retired Union Alpha preview, so no verified Union Alpha chart is published.

Blockers and remaining unknowns

  • R2 had no OPENROUTER_API_KEY or OPENCODE_API_KEY; no account, subscription, key or purchase was created.
  • There is no R2 evidence of old-ID HTTP behavior, OpenCode authorization, routing, latency, tool/image support, reliability or capacity.
  • Historical stealth retention wording must not be projected onto current Pareto without a Pareto-specific source. Go’s 0-day retention/no-training entry remains route-specific documentation.
  • The relationship between the old preview, paid Pareto and Go alias remains unverified at a version/snapshot level.
