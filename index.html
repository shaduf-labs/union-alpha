R4 route and method audit · 23 Sep 2026 UTC

Union Alpha remains retired; Pareto is a separate current route

OpenRouter’s historical Union Alpha page says the preview was revealed as Pareto by Unbiased and that the free period ended. The current catalog lists paid unbiased/pareto, but this does not establish a fixed preview snapshot. The former OpenCode Go alias is absent from the inspected docs, catalog, and changelog. R4 sent zero requests.

Decision
Do not integrate either former identifier. Do not use stealth/union-alpha or opencode-go/union-alpha. Treat paid Pareto as a separate service that needs its own approved evaluation and current data-terms review.
